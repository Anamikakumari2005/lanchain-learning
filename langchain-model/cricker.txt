On a sunny field so wide,
With hopes and dreams side by side,
A bat swings strong, a ball takes flight,
Crowds cheer loud with pure delight.

The bowler runs with steady pace,
Determination on his face,
The batsman stands, calm and still,
Ready to test his skill and will.

A crack of bat, the ball soars high,
Racing across the open sky,
Four or six, the crowd goes wild,
Every moment feels so styled.

From wickets falling to runs that grow,
The game’s excitement starts to flow,
Victory comes with sweat and fight,
Cricket shines in every light.

A game of passion, heart, and art,
Where every player plays their part,
More than just a sport we see,
It’s a feeling, it’s cricket’s beauty.
